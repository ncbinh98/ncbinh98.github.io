### Exploring Bai Dau Vung Tau: A Pristine Stopover Full of Charm 🌊

If you're planning a trip to Vung Tau, take the time to visit Bai Dau - a destination that still keeps its pristine beauty and hasn't been heavily exploited. In this article, we'll share everything about Bai Dau Vung Tau, from how to get there and the ideal time to visit, to the fun activities you shouldn't miss.


![Evening scenery painting](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-03-1633574061.jpg "Evening scenery painting")
---

#### 1. Introduction to Bai Dau Vung Tau 🏖️

Bai Dau is located on Tran Phu Street, a large and famous road in Vung Tau. According to local people, Bai Dau's former name was Bai Vung May (Foggy Bay Beach), because the natural scenery is covered with lots of forest mist. The place stands out with its pristine beauty, few visitors, and little exploitation.

---

#### 2. Directions to Bai Dau Vung Tau 🗺️

##### 2.1. By Car 🚗

If you start from Ho Chi Minh City, you can take the Long Thanh - Dau Giay expressway, turn onto National Route QL15 and continue to Vung Tau City. You can also follow Hoang Sa Street to cross Long Son Island, shortening the travel time to about 2 hours.

##### 2.2. By Motorbike 🏍️

You have two options: take National Route 1 then turn onto QL15, or cross the Cat Lai ferry, turn onto road DT769 and then reach QL15. After that, find Tran Phu Street along the coast and travel about 3km more to reach Bai Dau.

---

#### 3. The Ideal Time to Explore Bai Dau ☀️

Vung Tau's climate is mild, suitable for travelling all year round. However, the dry season (from May to October) is the best time because the sea water is clear and clean, the sea is calm with few underwater currents. Around 4pm is the perfect time to admire the gorgeous sunset here.

---

#### 4. Fun Activities at Bai Dau Vung Tau 🌟

##### 4.1. Enjoy the Peaceful Space of the Sea 🌴

You can relax on the fine sand, listen to the sound of the waves and gaze at the clear blue sky. Along the coast, there are rows of stone benches where you can stop and enjoy the view.

![Vung Tau scenery](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-04-1633509965.jpg "Vung Tau scenery")


##### 4.2. Instagram-Worthy Check-in at Tran Phu Alley 📸

Tran Phu Alley is located at 107-109 Tran Phu, with its unique sea view and old houses, making it the ideal place for "million-like" photos.

![Tran Phu instagrammable alley](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-05-1633509965.jpg "Tran Phu instagrammable alley")

##### 4.3. Visit the Lady of the Sea Church in Bai Dau ⛪

The shrine of the Virgin Mary with its majestic architecture and the 32-meter-tall statue of Mary is a sacred destination you can't miss. You can also conquer the summit of Nui Lon (Big Mountain) along 14 Stations of the Cross.

![Lady of the Sea church Bai Dau](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-07-1633574258.jpg "Lady of the Sea church Bai Dau")

##### 4.4. Discover Tempting Seafood Cuisine 🍤

Enjoy fresh seafood at famous restaurants such as Ganh Hao or Ngoc Dung, which offer rich menus and airy spaces right by the shore.

![Fish hotpot](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-08-1633509966.jpg "Fish hotpot")

##### 4.5. Try Fishing at Bai Dau 🎣

Fishing at the rocky reefs and deeper waters of Bai Dau will bring you an interesting experience. Choose the late afternoon time to enjoy the sea breeze and the beautiful sunset scenery.

![Fishing activity](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-09-1633509966.jpg "Fishing activity")

---

#### 5. Gorgeous Check-in Photos 📷

Bai Dau is not only a place to relax but also an ideal check-in spot with its deep blue sea scenery and mesmerizing photos.

![Check-in](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/bai-dau-vung-tau-diem-dung-chan-hoang-so-nhung-lai-khien-bao-nguoi-thuong-nho-12-1633579827.jpg "Check-in")

#### 6. Some Notes When Travelling to Bai Dau 📝

- Avoid going on rainy days when the sea is rough and dangerous.
- Be careful when checking in on the rocky reefs.
- Keep your personal belongings safe as there is no luggage storage service.
- Bring your own equipment if you want to organize an outdoor barbecue party.

Bai Dau Vung Tau still preserves its pristine beauty and offers special experiences. Plan your visit before this place becomes crowded!
