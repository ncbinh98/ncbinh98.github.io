# Explore Con Heo Hill Vung Tau: A Detailed Guide for Travelers

![Panoramic view of Con Heo Hill, Vung Tau](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/09/doi-con-heo-1.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Panoramic view of Con Heo Hill, Vung Tau")

![Panoramic view of Con Heo Hill, Vung Tau](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/09/doi-con-heo-2.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Panoramic view of Con Heo Hill, Vung Tau")

## About Con Heo Hill Vung Tau 🏞️
Con Heo Hill (Pig Hill) is located in the Bai Sau area of Vung Tau and is famous for its wild, untouched scenery and stunning sea views. It's a check-in spot you simply can't miss when traveling to Vung Tau.

## Where Does the Name "Con Heo Hill" Come From? ❓
The origin of the name remains a mystery, with two main stories. One says it comes from a poor neighborhood where children jokingly pointed out a pig (heo) seen on the hill. The other says that because of the cool seasonal "heo may" winds blowing across, locals called it Doi Heo May (Heo May Hill), which later evolved into Con Heo Hill (Pig Hill).

## When Is the Best Time to Visit Con Heo Hill? 🕒
The scenery at Con Heo Hill changes with the seasons. In the dry season the grass and trees turn golden; in the rainy season everything becomes lush and green. Especially at the end of the year, cherry blossoms bloom in full glory, drawing in many visitors.

![When is the best time to visit Con Heo Hill?](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/09/doi-con-heo-3.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "When is the best time to visit Con Heo Hill?")

## How to Find Your Way Up Con Heo Hill 📍
From Ho Chi Minh City, you can take a motorbike, bus, hydrofoil, or car to Vung Tau. From Vung Tau, follow Thuy Van Street, turn onto Phan Chu Trinh Street and continue straight into alley 222.

## What to Do Once You Reach Con Heo Hill? 🌅
- **Enjoy the stunning sea view in the distance**: From Con Heo Hill, you can see the full panorama of Bai Sau, Hon Ba islet and the horizon.
- **Snap "million-like" photos from countless angles**:
  - **The median divider area**: A safe and beautiful view.
  - **The rocky outcrop jutting over Bai Sau**: Capture the whole panorama of Bai Sau.
  - **The hilltop corner facing the sea**: An ultra-chill sea view.
  - **The big-rock corner at the summit**: Huge rock formations as your backdrop.
  - **The golden grass slopes in the dry season**: A dreamy, poetic scene.
  - **Do mai blossom season**: A romantic sea of pink flowers.

## Watch the Sunrise and Sunset 🌄
Con Heo Hill is an ideal spot for watching sunrise and sunset. Don't forget to capture these moments.

## What to Eat When Visiting Vung Tau? 🍽️
- **Goi Ca Mai (mackerel salad)**: Fresh mackerel cleaned and marinated with garlic, chili, vinegar and toasted rice powder.
- **Lau Ca Duoi (ray fish hotpot)**: Ray fish simmered with vegetables in a sweet-and-sour broth.
- **Oc Vu Nang (Vú Nàng snails)**: A rare seafood, steamed or boiled to preserve its natural flavor.
- **Bun Be Be (mantis shrimp noodle soup)**: Mantis shrimp cooked with squid, shrimp, tomatoes and tofu.

![Goi Ca Mai (mackerel salad)](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/09/doi-con-heo-13.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Goi Ca Mai (mackerel salad)")

## Important Tips for Visiting Con Heo Hill ⚠️
- The road up the hill is very steep and narrow, so drive carefully.
- Wear sneakers and comfortable clothes.
- There are several drink stalls, but you can also bring your own water and light snacks.
- Don't climb over the median divider — it's very dangerous.
- Keep the place clean; don't litter.
- Visit early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the harsh sun.
- Motorbike and car parking is available on site.
