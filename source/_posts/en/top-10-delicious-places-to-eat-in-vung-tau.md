# Top 10 Delicious Places To Eat When Visiting Vung Tau 🏖️

Vung Tau is not only famous for its beautiful beaches but also for its rich culinary paradise. Below is a list of 10 delicious restaurants that you cannot miss when visiting Vung Tau.

## 1. NHÀ HÀNG GÀNH HÀO 🍽️

- **Address 1:** 03 Trần Phú, Phường 5, TP. Vũng Tàu
- **Address 2:** Khu vực cảng tàu khách cánh ngầm, 09 Hạ Long, Phường 2, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Gành Hào Restaurant](https://ganhhao.com.vn/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/canh_ganh_hao.jpg "Gành Hào Restaurant 1")

![Gành Hào Restaurant](https://www.dulichthienthai.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/nha-hang-ganh-hao-2.jpg "Gành Hào Restaurant 2")

Gành Hào Restaurant is famous for fresh, delicious seafood, serving all day with a diverse menu from breakfast and lunch to dinner. In particular, the restaurant also has a ground-floor space for coffee and breakfast.

## 2. ỐC TỰ NHIÊN VŨNG TÀU - ỐC TỰ NHIÊN 4 🦪

- **Address:** 32 Lê Quý Đôn, Phường 1, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Oc Tu Nhien Vung Tau](https://static.riviu.co/image/2020/11/30/1a3f7973398b96cafeceeeac167c9759_output.jpeg "Oc Tu Nhien Vung Tau")

Ốc Tự Nhiên is a familiar spot for shellfish lovers. Here, you can enjoy countless types of fresh snails prepared in many different ways.

## 3. BUFFET LẨU 130K THỐNG NHẤT 🍲

- **Address:** 397 Thống Nhất Mới, Phường 8, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Thong Nhat Hotpot Buffet](https://i.ibb.co/VgtTRWV/287507851-5167674866656989-117525869938845327-n.jpg "Thong Nhat Hotpot Buffet")

For only 130K, you can enjoy unlimited hotpot varieties at Buffet Lẩu Thống Nhất. This is an ideal destination for those who want to enjoy many kinds of hotpot at an affordable price.

## 4. TIỆM LẨU BUFFET & TIỆM NƯỚNG Ở ĐÂY 🍢

- **Address:** 19 Thủ Khoa Huân, Phường 1, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Tiem Lau Buffet](https://i.ibb.co/C86pN8j/344335590-751878533074457-8087531941779890824-n.jpg "Tiem Lau Buffet")

Tiệm Lẩu Buffet & Tiệm Nướng Ở Đây is the ideal place to enjoy a rich variety of hotpot and grilled dishes. The cozy space and attentive service will make you feel comfortable.

## 5. LẨU CÁ ĐUỐI HOÀNG MINH 🐟

- **Address:** 44 Trương Công Định, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Ray Fish Hotpot Hoang Minh](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thuong-thuc-dac-san-binh-dan-tai-nha-hang-lau-ca-duoi-truong-cong-dinh-1-1633971708.jpg "Ray Fish Hotpot Hoang Minh")

Lẩu Cá Đuối Hoàng Minh (ray fish hotpot) is a signature dish of Vung Tau. With fresh ingredients and a rich hotpot broth, this is a spot you cannot miss.

## 6. QUÁN BÁNH KHỌT GỐC VÚ SỮA VŨNG TÀU 🥞

- **Address:** 14 Nguyễn Trường Tộ, Phường 3, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Banh Khot Goc Vu Sua](https://cdn.alongwalk.info/vn/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/22025658/image-banh-khot-vung-tau-o-dau-ngon-nhi-chia-se-12-quan-banh-khot-ngon-o-vung-tau-165581621882308.png "Banh Khot Goc Vu Sua")

Bánh Khọt Gốc Vú Sữa is famous for its crispy, crunchy Banh Khot with sweet fresh shrimp filling inside. It's a rustic yet very distinctive dish of Vung Tau.

## 7. QUÁN BÁNH BÈO TUYẾT MAI VŨNG TÀU 🍛

- **Address:** Số 9 Phan Chu Trinh, Phường 2, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Banh Beo Tuyet Mai](https://media-cdn.tripadvisor.com/media/photo-s/09/eb/d2/a0/tuyet-mai-vung-tau.jpg "Banh Beo Tuyet Mai")

Quán Bánh Bèo Tuyết Mai brings you the traditional Banh Beo flavor with a rich dipping sauce and plentiful toppings.

## 8. CƠM TẤM LOAN 🍚

- **Address:** 78 Lê Lai, Phường 3, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Com Tam Loan](https://i.ibb.co/JpVzX9P/295624877-108635018597669-5343562745175075935-n.jpg "Com Tam Loan")

Cơm Tấm Loan is an ideal stop for broken rice lovers. With fragrant grilled pork, soft fluffy rice and a rich homemade fish sauce, the restaurant has attracted many diners.

## 9. CƠM TẤM 67 🍖

- **Address:** 88 Lê Lai, Phường 3, TP. Vũng Tàu

![Com Tam 67](https://ticovilla.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Com-tam-Vung-Tau-3.jpg "Com Tam 67")

Cơm Tấm 67 is also a solid choice for broken rice fans. The restaurant is famous for its fragrant grilled ribs marinated with signature spices.

## 10. TRỨNG LÒNG ĐÀO & BÁNH FLAN NGỌN HẢI ĐĂNG 🍮

Your food experience at Ngọn Hải Đăng (the Lighthouse) will be even more exciting with soft-boiled eggs and smooth, sweet flan.

![Soft Boiled Eggs & Flan](https://vungtaureview.vn/attachments/a215253e-6d36-4018-9c63-6f84208b4fb3-jpeg.5214/ "Soft Boiled Eggs & Flan")
