# Explore NBNB Bakery & Cafe Vung Tau – Hot Trend Caramel Donuts And Super Hot Pizza Bomb 🍩☕

Are you a fan of pastries and milk tea? Looking for a bakery with a fancy space for your weekend date? Let us introduce NBNB Bakery & Cafe – the bakery taking Vung Tau by storm with hot trend caramel donuts and a brand-new branch just opened on Binh Gia street!

## 🍩 Hot Trend Caramel Donut – A Best Seller You Can't Miss

![Hot Trend Caramel Donut at NBNB Bakery](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30097-ac2c53dfb477c2debca6a4ab969174db.jpg "Hot Trend Caramel Donut at NBNB Bakery & Cafe Vung Tau")

The signature item that made NBNB famous is the **Hot Trend Caramel Donut** line with 3 tempting flavors:
- 🍩 **Traditional** – sweet original caramel flavor
- 🍵 **Matcha** – rich and creamy Japanese green tea flavor
- 🍫 **Tiramisu** – rich Italian style

Besides that, the bakery also has the **6-lobe bread** (bánh mì 6 múi) that ladies absolutely love – we guarantee no other place in Vung Tau has it!

## 🍕 Pizza Bomb – A Unique New Dish In Vung Tau

![Pepperoni cheese Pizza Bomb at NBNB](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30098-0594317feda34ff9324599cd7bc202b8.jpg "Pizza Bomb with pepperoni and cheese imported from America")

If you think a bakery only has sweets, you're wrong! **Pizza Bomb** is the new dish "going viral" at the shop. Pepperoni and cheese imported from America, with a taste no less than pizza at fancy restaurants. Priced at only **35K** – what a bargain, right?

![Cute little cream puffs at NBNB](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30099-0c899d763aa50dc30477325d999d4541.jpg "Crispy cream puffs at NBNB Bakery & Cafe")

## 🎃 Japanese-Technology Pumpkin Bread

The pumpkin bread is made using Japanese technology, using **real pumpkin** to make the bread, so it stays naturally soft and moist, with no additives or preservatives. A healthy choice for those who love a light, natural sweetness!

![Various cakes at NBNB Bakery](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30100-9e8fc33b469068212928be4c7b8cafe4.jpg "Delicious baked goods at NBNB")

## 🍫 New York Chocolate & Cream Puffs – 15K Each

The **New York Chocolate** line has up to 5 different flavors at only **15K/each** – a super friendly price! And the small, cute **crispy cream puffs**, perfect for snacking, are a wonderful choice for your afternoon treat.

![Various cakes at NBNB Bakery](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30101-cf8fbb652c36e2c8c8454c4b542c2593.jpg "Sweet cakes in many flavors at NBNB")

## 🧋 Diverse Drinks – Tea And Coffee Both Available

You can't have cakes without drinks! NBNB has fruit tea, milk tea and even **tea-coffee mixes** – an unusual yet incredibly fragrant flavor. Anyone who loves trying new tastes must drop by!

![Various drinks at NBNB](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30102-4e8e257f7688fce71a35cd355e546abf.jpg "Milk tea and attractive drinks at NBNB")

## 🎉 Grand Opening Combo Deals – Only At The New Branch

Celebrating the opening of branch 2, NBNB has 2 super special combos:
- 💥 **Combo 1:** 2 Donuts + any 1 drink – only **65K**
- 💥 **Combo 2:** 2 milk teas + free 2 mini donuts

> Grand Opening sale lasts only **7 days** – hurry up and go early!

![Space of NBNB branch 2](https://vungtaureview.vn/data/attachments/30/30104-cdedb84070b2fd8d25655d663256246a.jpg "Spacious and airy new branch at Binh Gia")
