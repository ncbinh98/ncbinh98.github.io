## Vung Tau In 48 Hours: A Super Chill Beachfront Homestay Guide!

Planning a weekend "heat escape" to Vung Tau? As someone who has lived here for over 20 years, let me share all the secrets for an epic 48-hour trip — especially the cutest sea view homestays where you can take endless photos!

###  Getting To Vung Tau

* **Coach / Limousine:** Tickets from 200,000 - 300,000 VND per way, about 2 hours.
* **Hydrofoil:** A fun experience — enjoy sea views along the way.
* **Private vehicle:** Flexible timing, great for friend groups or families.

###  A "Real Vung Tau" Breakfast

* **Bun rieu tom (crab noodle soup) at Thuan Phuc, squid hu tieu at Hoang Hoa Tham:** From 30,000 - 50,000 VND per bowl.
* **Fish cake banh mi:** A quick and convenient option.
* **Sea view coffee:** Morning coffee at cafes along Ha Long and Tran Phu streets.

![](https://camnangdulich.net/travel/Cache/Uploads/camnangdulich/2024/thang3/soho-coffee-vung-tau.jpg "Vung Tau coffee")

### ⛪ Visit The Christ The King Statue

* The symbol of Vung Tau, on top of Nui Nho (Small Mountain).
* Enjoy a panoramic view of the seaside city from above.
* Near Mui Nghinh Phong — easy to combine sightseeing.

![](https://i1-dulich.vnecdn.net/2024/05/03/Vung-Tau-6034-1714726487.jpg?w=680&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=WLkXKd2z8hZXTTJJAJVW2w "Christ the King statue Vung Tau")

###  Check Into A "Million-Dollar" Sea View Homestay

* Pick a homestay or hotel along the coast on Ha Long, Thuy Van or Tran Phu streets.
* Prices range from budget to luxury — something for every wallet.
* Enjoy a comfortable, well-equipped retreat.

![](https://drscdn.500px.org/photo/1093681090/q%3D90_m%3D2048/v2?sig=bd6d9426ced272bc11fa5286e6b8489ca296ffd4c4c7840d5e6d66ca449c6d8f "Vung Tau homestay")

###  Feast On Vung Tau Food

* **Banh Khot:** Ba Hai, Co Ba, Ut Loan... take your pick!
* **Seafood:** Oc Tu Nhien, Oc Thien Nhien... fresh and reasonably priced.
* **Deep-fried baby squid with fish sauce (Ganh Hao style):** A must-try in Vung Tau.

![](https://www.huongnghiepaau.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/mon-banh-khot.jpg "Vung Tau Banh Khot")

###  Swim And Chill At Sunset

* Bai Truoc, Bai Sau, Bai Dau: pick your favorite beach.
* Watch a romantic sunset at Ganh Hao.
* Explore the Vung Tau night market or seaside bars.

![](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/bai-truoc-1-768x510.jpg "Vung Tau beach")

###  Explore Vung Tau By Bicycle

* Rent a bike and ride around the city, enjoying the fresh air.
* Visit Bach Dinh — admire the old French architecture and the view of Bai Truoc.

![](https://datviettour.com.vn/uploads/images/mien-nam/vung-tau/danh-thang/bach-dinh-vung-tau.jpg "Bach Dinh Vung Tau")

###  Experience Long Son Floating Village

* Discover a traditional fishing village and learn about aquaculture.
* Enjoy fresh seafood on the rafts — especially oysters and blood cockles.

![](https://vielimousine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/lang-be-long-son-1-1024x550.jpg "Long Son floating village")

###  Other Experiences To Try

* The lighthouse, Ho May, the Upside Down House...
* Try being a helicopter pilot.
* Relax at resorts in Xuyen Moc, Binh Chau, Ho Tram.

##  Golden Tips For Your Trip

* Book your homestay/hotel in advance, especially on weekends and holidays.
* Bring sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses to protect your skin.
* Pack swimwear and personal items...
* Research food and entertainment spots in advance to save time.
* Rent a motorbike or car for flexible travel.

Have a wonderful and memorable trip to Vung Tau!
