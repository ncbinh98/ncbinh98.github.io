# Experience Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau: The Ideal Landmark Just Steps From Sunbeam Homestay!

🌅 Hi everyone! As a travel enthusiast living in Vung Tau, I want to share with you a truly special landmark – Thap Tam Thang. The best part is that this new iconic structure sits very close to Sunbeam Homestay, just a few steps away!

## 🏝️ Discover Thap Tam Thang – Vung Tau's New Icon

![thap-tam-thang-toan-canh](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-2-1756955219.jpg "Panoramic view of Thap Tam Thang")

Thap Tam Thang is a majestic architectural landmark inaugurated on the National Day of September 2, located right on Thuy Van Street within Back Beach Park. This prime spot sits at the intersection of Le Hong Phong and Thuy Van streets, with one side facing straight out to the dreamy Back Beach.

## 📖 The Historical Meaning Behind the Unique Architecture

![y-nghia-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-vung-tau-3-1756514953.jpg "The architectural meaning of Thap Tam Thang")

Thap Tam Thang draws inspiration from the three ancient villages of Thang Nhat, Thang Nhi and Thang Tam – the very roots of Vung Tau as a city. Designed by Italian architect Oliviero Godi, it tells the story of how the villages were founded back in the 19th century.

## 🛵 Getting to Thap Tam Thang Is Super Easy

![di-chuyen-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-vung-tau-4-1756514953.jpg "Getting to Thap Tam Thang")

From Sunbeam Homestay, it only takes you 5-10 minutes on foot to reach Thap Tam Thang! If you're staying further away, it's about 2km from the Vung Tau bus station, or from the Martyrs Monument roundabout, turn onto Le Hong Phong Street heading toward the sea. Motorbikes are very convenient with a spacious parking lot, or you can simply take a taxi.

## 🏗️ Unique Architecture Like a Giant Ship

![kien-truc-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-6-1756957349.jpg "The architecture of Thap Tam Thang")

Thap Tam Thang consists of 143 polygonal pillars, of which 3 main pillars range from 10.9m to 31.6m high, symbolizing the three ancient villages. The design resembles ship bows heading out to the open sea, expressing the aspiration to reach further. The tops of the pillars are clad in mosaic ceramic tiles, creating a stunning light effect.

## 🌙 A Space of Artistic Lights and Water Music

![dem-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-7-1758012196.jpg "Thap Tam Thang at night")

At night, Thap Tam Thang drapes itself in a brilliant beauty with its multicolored artistic lighting system. The captivating water music area is the perfect place for families and children to play and unwind after a long day.

## 📸 Grab These "Instagrammable" Check-in Spots Now

### 📷 Low-Angle Wide Shot
![goc-rong-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-15-1758013558.jpg "Wide-angle shot of Thap Tam Thang")

Stand close to the base of the tower and tilt your camera up – you'll instantly get a photo that emphasizes the grandeur of the structure. This angle is especially impressive when light streams through the gaps, creating a beautiful effect.

### 🌅 Artistic Backlight Photography
![nguoc-sang-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-14-1758013557.jpg "Backlight shot of Thap Tam Thang")

From 11am to 2pm is the golden window for backlight shots. The sunlight casts sharp shadows, giving birth to photos full of artistry and depth.

### 🖼️ Close-Up Shots Between the Pillars
![can-canh-thap-tam-thang](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/thap-tam-thang-18-1758013558.jpg "Close-up shot of Thap Tam Thang")

Check in between the giant pillars with their bright white tile cladding for an ultra-cool background. Pro tip: wear dark or bold-colored outfits to stand out against the scene.
