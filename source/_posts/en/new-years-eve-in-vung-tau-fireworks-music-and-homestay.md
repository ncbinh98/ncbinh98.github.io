# A Dazzling New Year's Eve in Vung Tau with Fireworks and a Grand Concert🎆

🌟 **Vung Tau** is gearing up for a colorful and vibrant New Year's Eve with a **fireworks display** combined with a **grand concert** on the night of January 28, 2025. This is a wonderful opportunity for you and your family to enjoy the festive atmosphere and find a **homestay near Front Beach** for easy access to the event.

## 🎉 **New Year's Eve Event Schedule**
- **19:00 - 22:00**: Kick off with a **Lion Dance** and **Festival Drum** performance setting a Vietnamese record, along with performances by singers, modern dance groups, and bands.
- **22:00 - 24:00**: The **"True Connections"** grand concert featuring famous artists such as **Quang Hung MasterD**, **Kay Tran**, and **DJ Rin**.
- **0:00**: A dazzling **fireworks display** at **Front Beach**, ushering in a hopeful Year of the Snake 2025.

---

## 🏖️ **Event Venues**
- **Front Beach**: The main area for the fireworks display, opposite Thong Nhat Street and the cluster 5 land area.
- **Quang Trung Flower Garden**: The venue for arts programs and the grand concert.
