### Top 12 Most Instagrammable Photo Spots In Vung Tau 📸

#### 1. Christ The King Statue ✝️

![Christ The King Statue](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/1-23.png "Christ The King Statue")
The Christ the King statue is one of Vung Tau's most iconic landmarks. To reach it, you need to climb 1000 steps. The top of Nho Mountain rewards you not only with a giant statue but also a stunning panoramic view of the city.

#### 2. The Lighthouse 🏯

![The Lighthouse](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/6-21.png "The Lighthouse")
Vung Tau Lighthouse, with its unique architecture and vintage white paint, is a must-visit spot. The winding roads and lush green trees create a vivid backdrop for every photo.

#### 3. Mui Nghinh Phong 🌊

![Mui Nghinh Phong](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/20-9.png "Mui Nghinh Phong")
Mui Nghinh Phong is famous for its "heaven's gate", an ideal place for photos. The scenery here is a perfect blend of nature and humanity, producing beautiful and artistic shots.

#### 4. Ho Coc 🏖️

![Ho Coc](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/22-9.png "Ho Coc")

![Ho Coc](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/24-5.png "Ho Coc")
Ho Coc offers a peaceful and pristine space, perfect for those who love tranquility and nature. Photos taken here always carry a magical, fairy-tale vibe.

#### 5. Ho Da Xanh 🏞️

![Ho Da Xanh](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/25-5.png "Ho Da Xanh")
Ho Da Xanh is famous for its gorgeous natural scenery and emerald blue lake. It's an ideal spot for wedding photos or artistic shots.

#### 6. Con Heo Hill 🌿

![Con Heo Hill](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/29-3.png "Con Heo Hill")
Con Heo Hill, with its open and rugged landscape, creates unique and edgy photos. From here, you can admire the whole of Vung Tau.

#### 7. Upside Down House 🏠

![Upside Down House](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/37-2.png "Upside Down House")
![Upside Down House](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/40-1.png "Upside Down House")
The Upside Down House is a unique photo spot in Vung Tau. Everything inside is flipped upside down, creating fun and quirky photos.

#### 8. Suoi Nghe Sheep Hill 🐑

![Suoi Nghe Sheep Hill](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/42.png "Suoi Nghe Sheep Hill")
Suoi Nghe Sheep Hill gives you the chance to take photos with gentle sheep in a pristine and peaceful setting.

#### 9. Marina Yacht Pier ⛵

![Marina Yacht Pier](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/45.png "Marina Yacht Pier")

![Marina Yacht Pier](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/47.png "Marina Yacht Pier")
Marina Yacht Pier, with its colorful sailboats, is an ideal place for modern Western-style photos. You can even charter a yacht out to sea for even better shots.

#### 10. Zenna Pool Camp 🏕️

![Zenna Pool Camp](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/48.png "Zenna Pool Camp")
Zenna Pool Camp is an ideal spot for camping and photography. With its open space and the blue sea nearby, you'll have a wonderful experience here.

#### 11. Bach Dinh 🏛️

![Bach Dinh](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/52.png "Bach Dinh")
Bach Dinh is a mansion with classic European architecture, surrounded by white frangipani trees that make a lovely backdrop. It's the perfect place for elegant and refined photos.

#### 12. The Road At Bai Sau 🌸

![The Road At Bai Sau](https://cdn.vntrip.vn/cam-nang/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/55.png "The Road At Bai Sau")
Thuy Van Street at Bai Sau has a poetic and modern beauty. This is an ideal location for beautiful and impressive photos.
