# Explore Nui Nho Vung Tau: An Experience You Can't Miss 🏠

## Where Is Nui Nho Vung Tau Located? 📍

![Nui Nho Vung Tau](https://file.hstatic.net/1000169629/file/vung_tau_a5b6ddbb954140c0941c48e0273e18b5.jpg "Nui Nho Vung Tau")
**Address:** Ward 2, Vung Tau City, Ba Ria – Vung Tau Province  
Nui Nho (Small Mountain), also known as Tao Phung Mountain, lies in the south of Vung Tau. It rises about 170 meters and covers an area of 120 hectares. At the foot of the mountain, you'll find a coastal road lined with well-developed tourism services, where you can stop and rest at beautiful homestays and hotels. Follow Nui Nho to the end and you'll reach Nghinh Phong peak, home to Bai Huong Phong and Bai Vong Nguyet – two famous, gorgeous swimming beaches.

## How to Get Up Nui Nho Vung Tau 🚗

![Road to Nui Nho](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui3.jpg "Road to Nui Nho")
Start from the center of Vung Tau, follow Ba Muoi Thang Tu Street, turn onto Pham Hong Thai, continue to the end of Le Loi Street, then turn right onto Hoang Dieu Street to reach Bai Truoc park. From there, continue along Ha Long Street and you'll arrive at the foot of Nui Nho.

## Unforgettable Experiences on Nui Nho Vung Tau 🌄

### 1. Enjoy the Beauty of the Road to the Lighthouse 🚶‍♂️

![Vung Tau Lighthouse](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui2.jpg "Vung Tau Lighthouse")
The Vung Tau Lighthouse sits along a dreamlike road. From here, you can take photos with the historic lighthouse and admire the sea and the city from above. Especially in April and May, you'll see white cotton-tree fluff blooming everywhere, creating a truly spectacular scene.

### 2. Visit the Oldest Lighthouse in Southeast Asia 🏰

![Old Lighthouse](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui4.jpg "Old Lighthouse")
Built in 1870, the Vung Tau Lighthouse is one of the oldest lighthouses in Southeast Asia. Here, you can tour the lighthouse keepers' quarters and take in a panoramic view of Vung Tau from above.

### 3. Admire the Christ the King Statue ✝️

![Christ the King Statue](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui5.jpg "Christ the King Statue")
The 32-meter Christ the King Statue stands atop Tao Phung Mountain. To reach it, you'll need to climb 793 steps. From the statue's arms, you can enjoy a sweeping view of the city and the vast blue sea.

### 4. Savor Co Tien Yaourt 🍦

![Co Tien Yaourt](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui6.jpg "Co Tien Yaourt")
On your way back, don't forget to stop by the Co Tien yaourt shop to enjoy its famous yogurt and soft-boiled eggs. The treats here will refresh you after a long day of sightseeing.

## Tips for Visiting Nui Nho 📝

![Tips for visiting Nui Nho](https://halotravel.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/nui7.jpg "Tips for visiting Nui Nho")

- Dress lightly and comfortably, and wear sneakers.
- Park your vehicle at the foot of the lighthouse for 5,000 VND per ticket; admission is free.
- Drive carefully and keep your personal belongings safe.
- Visit in the evening to see the whole city sparkling with lights.
