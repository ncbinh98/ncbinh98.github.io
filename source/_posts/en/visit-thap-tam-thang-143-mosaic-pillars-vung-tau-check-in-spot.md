# Visit Thap Tam Thang – 143 Mosaic Pillars Drawing Crowds by the Vung Tau Sea 📸🌊

Have you ever scrolled through social media and spotted photos taken among a forest of shimmering white pillars, with the blue sea behind? 😍 That's **Thap Tam Thang in Vung Tau** – a landmark that has been open for more than a year yet is still one of the hottest check-in spots on Back Beach (Bãi Sau). Today we're sharing an updated guide with everything you need to know before you go!

## 🌟 Still Trending After a Year – Why Does Thap Tam Thang Draw Such Crowds?

Thap Tam Thang stands right on Thuy Van Street, next to Back Beach, with a total investment of **155 billion VND**. Surprisingly, it took only **75 days** to build and officially opened at the end of August 2025.

Today, Thap Tam Thang holds **4.7/5 stars from more than 3,200 Google reviews** ⭐ – a score few attractions in Vung Tau can match. Visitors praise its striking architecture, spacious square and seaside location the most.

![Visitors checking in among the pillars of Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/27/wm1-vne03089-1793073251-1793073307.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=kr6p2ca1Oxszbc5_tEvEPg "The pillars of Thap Tam Thang attract visitors for check-in photos")

Thanh Hai – a visitor on his first trip to the tower with his girlfriend – said he was impressed by the scale of the structure, the open space and the many photo angles: *"Only when you come here do you understand why so many people share this place on social media."* We hear the same thing from plenty of our guests at Sunbeam too! 😄

## ⛵ Three Boat Bows Heading Out to Sea – The Story Behind the 143 Pillars

Looking in from the square, you'll see **143 pillars ranging from 4.55 m to 34 m tall**, arranged in clusters that rise toward the sea. Together they evoke **three boat bows** cutting through the waves 🌊

![Aerial view of Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau beside Back Beach](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/27/wm1-dji-0120-2-1793070286-1793070452.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=TK_LivXptJEkLJ8YfP4WtA "Panorama of Thap Tam Thang on Back Beach, Vung Tau")

The three bows represent **Thang Nhat, Thang Nhi and Thang Tam** – the three ancient villages that grew into today's Vung Tau. For a deeper look at the history and design concept, read our earlier post [Experience Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau](/en/explore-thap-tam-thang-vung-tau-near-sunbeam-homestay/), written when the tower first opened.

![The 143 pillars of Thap Tam Thang rising toward the Vung Tau sea](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/30/wm1-vne03022-1793361602-1793362040.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=dC404zKQBfaxSilRCvx2dQ "Pillar clusters of Thap Tam Thang evoking three boat bows heading out to sea")

## 📸 Get Lost in a "Maze" of Mosaic Pillars

What makes Thap Tam Thang a photo paradise is its **mosaic-tiled pillars**, set in an interlocking pattern that creates narrow, maze-like paths. As you wander between them, you'll keep discovering new angles ✨

![Visitors strolling among the mosaic pillars of Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/30/wm1-vne03043-1793361806-1793362040.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=My-4lTOeXrzGeC4t2bC0kQ "Interlocking paths between the mosaic pillars of Thap Tam Thang")

A few quick tips for great Thap Tam Thang check-in photos:

- 👗 **Outfit:** Go for dark or bold colors (red, yellow, deep blue) to stand out against the white pillars
- 📐 **Angle:** Stand close to the base of a pillar and shoot upward to capture the tower's height
- 🚶 **Moment:** Shots taken mid-stride between two rows of pillars look more natural and have more depth

## 🌅 Golden Hours – When Is the Best Time to Visit?

Based on many visitors' experience, the best times to visit Thap Tam Thang are **early morning or late afternoon**. The light is soft, the weather is cooler and photos come out beautifully without harsh glare.

![Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau in soft afternoon light](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/30/wm1-vne03161-1793361809-1793362040.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=WWieVf7LQ7v7L9oqMWV0Dg "Visit Thap Tam Thang early morning or late afternoon for the best photos")

⚠️ On very sunny days, remember to **avoid going at midday** – the square is wide with little shade, and the tiles reflect a lot of sunlight. Our tip: catch the sunrise on Back Beach and drop by the tower right after – it's cool and far less crowded!

## 🏖️ Eyes on Back Beach and the 3.2 km Thuy Van Promenade

Beyond the pillars themselves, Thap Tam Thang is designed to **draw your gaze toward Back Beach**. Stand in the middle of the tower and look out – a wide stretch of sea and sky opens up before you 💙

![Thap Tam Thang framing the view of Back Beach Vung Tau](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/27/wm1-vne03052-1793073266-1793073307.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=xK8omAnKseJWo1rIvtQ9ew "View of Back Beach Vung Tau from Thap Tam Thang")

The tower is the architectural highlight of the Back Beach renovation project. Along the **3.2 km seaside stretch of Thuy Van Street**, there are now parks, squares and event spaces – perfect for walking, cycling or a morning run. Not sure which beach to swim at? ## 🌈 After Dark – Colorful Lights and Water Music

By day Thap Tam Thang is pure white, but at night it transforms with **multicolored lighting and a water music area** 🎶 This is when locals and visitors flock to the square for an evening stroll, and kids have a blast playing around the fountains.

![Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau glowing with colorful lights at night](https://i1-vnexpress.vnecdn.net/2026/10/27/wm1-thap-1-2-1793073531-1793073996.jpg?w=1200&h=0&q=100&dpr=1&fit=crop&s=mvIqTHg-FAUUrsROPheNpQ "Thap Tam Thang lit up with colored lights and water music at night")

Our suggested evening plan: seafood dinner on Thuy Van Street ➝ walk to the tower to see the lights ➝ enjoy the sea breeze on Back Beach ➝ head back to the homestay to rest. Easy-going and super chill! 🌙
