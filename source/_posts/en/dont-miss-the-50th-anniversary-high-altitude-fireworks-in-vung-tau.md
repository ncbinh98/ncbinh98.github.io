# Don't Miss the 50th Anniversary High-Altitude Fireworks in Vung Tau – Close to Sunbeam Homestay 🎆✨

Do you have any plans for the evening of July 2 yet? If you're in Vung Tau or about to visit this beautiful coastal city, let me tell you about a spectacular event you simply can't miss!

## 🎆 A High-Altitude Fireworks Show Lighting Up the Vung Tau Sky

At exactly 9pm on July 2, Thap Tam Thang Square (Vung Tau ward) will be the centerpiece of a 15-minute high-altitude fireworks display organized by the Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee. This is an event to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the day Saigon – Gia Dinh officially had the honor of bearing the name of President Ho Chi Minh (July 2, 2026).

![High-altitude fireworks at Thap Tam Thang Vung Tau](https://i.ex-cdn.com/danviet.vn/files/content/2026/06/13/vung-tau_phao-hoa-0711.jpg "A dazzling fireworks display at Thap Tam Thang – Vung Tau")

Located right in the city center with an open sea view – you can easily admire the fireworks from many different angles. And the best part is that our **Sunbeam Homestay** is just a few minutes' walk away! Watch the fireworks to your heart's content, then head back to your room to relax – no traffic jams or long commutes to worry about.

## 🎉 A Lively Line-Up of Activities From Late June Through the End of July

It's not just the fireworks – all of Vung Tau is buzzing with a series of cultural, sports and entertainment events during this period. Let's run through the most notable activities together!

### 📸 Anniversary Photo Exhibition & Children's Book Fair
- **Photo exhibition:** July 1 – July 11 at the Revolutionary Tradition House (1 Ba Cu)
- **6th Ho Chi Minh City Children's Book Fair:** June 25 – July 5 at Thuy Van Park
- **Food industry week:** July 3 – July 12 at Tam Thang Square

### 🏃 Thrilling Community Runs
- **PVD Run:** June 26–27 at the 1 Ba Cu area
- **"Construction Newspaper 2026" Run:** June 27–28 around Thap Tam Thang

Both runs take place right near the city center, making it super convenient to combine exercise with exploring Vung Tau.

### 🧒 Activities for Children
- 2026 Summer Children's Chess Tournament
- Painting contest "I draw the city I love"
- Summer children's football tournament
- Reading Day festival
- 3 classes on drowning prevention skills

### 🎾 Pickleball Tournament & Reading Culture Space
- **Pickleball tournament:** July 4–5 at local training courts
- **Reading culture space & mobile book fair:** July 14–20 at 1 Ba Cu (in collaboration with Dong A Culture Joint Stock Company)
