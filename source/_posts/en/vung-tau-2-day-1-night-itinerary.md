# Vung Tau 2-Day 1-Night Itinerary: Enjoy The Perfect Getaway 🌊🌞

## A Glimpse Of Vung Tau 🏖️

Located just 125km southeast of Ho Chi Minh City, Vung Tau is an ideal destination for weekend trips. With a 42km coastline, Vung Tau is always lively and attracts crowds of visitors every summer. Its year-round cool climate and many beautiful sights make it the perfect choice to "escape" 🌴.

![Bai Truoc Vung Tau](<https://6.img.izshop.vn/tv31/images/2(32).jpg> "Bai Truoc Vung Tau")

## The Ideal Time To Visit Vung Tau 🌤️

You can visit Vung Tau at any time of the year. However, the best period is from November to April, when the weather is dry and convenient for outdoor activities.

## How To Get To Vung Tau 🚗🛵

### By Coach

- **Popular bus companies**: Hoa Mai, Toan Thang, Phuong Trang.
- **Ticket price**: 150,000 - 200,000 VND/person.
- **Travel time**: 2.5 - 3 hours.

### Self-Drive Car

- **Benefits**: Private, comfortable, flexible schedule.
- **Travel time**: 2 - 2.5 hours.

### By Motorbike

- **Routes**:
  - Route 1: From Cat Lai ferry to National Highway 51.
  - Route 2: Via National Highway 1A, Vung Tau intersection, National Highway 51.

![Road To Vung Tau](https://vietnamvivu.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/cac-cung-duong-phuot-tu-sai-gon-di-vung-tau-1.jpg "Road To Vung Tau")

## Vung Tau 2-Day 1-Night Itinerary 📅

### Day 1

- **5:30am**: Depart from Ho Chi Minh City.
- **8:00am**: Breakfast at Banh Canh Long Huong.
- **9:00am**: Visit Ba Ria - Vung Tau Museum.
- **11:00am**: Lunch at Com Nieu Dat Viet.
- **12:00pm**: Check in to the hotel and rest.
- **2:30pm**: Explore Vung Tau Lighthouse and enjoy yogurt with soft-boiled eggs.
- **4:00pm**: Swim and stroll around Bai Truoc.
- **5:30pm**: Rest at the hotel.
- **7:00pm**: Wander around the city, enjoy seafood at Oc Cam or explore the night market.

![Vung Tau Lighthouse](https://ittpa.baria-vungtau.gov.vn/portal/editor/images/Anh%20tin%20du%20lich/shhj123456wtrwwrwwrw1212.jpg "Vung Tau Lighthouse")

![Bai Truoc Vung Tau](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/09/bai-truoc-7.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Bai Truoc Vung Tau")

### Day 2

- **5:00am**: Watch the sunrise at Bai Sau.
- **7:00am**: Breakfast at Hu Tieu Muc - Ong Gia Cali.
- **8:00am**: Visit Tao Phung Park and the Christ the King statue.
- **1:00pm**: Check out and have lunch.
- **2:00pm**: Check in at the instagrammable alley No. 107 Tran Phu.
- **3:00pm**: Head back to Ho Chi Minh City.
