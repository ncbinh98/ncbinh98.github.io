# Budget-Smart Shopping Tips For Your Vung Tau Trip 🏡💰

Hello everyone! If you're planning a trip to Vung Tau and choosing a homestay as your ideal base, this shopping guide is a must-read. We've spent plenty of time in Vung Tau and want to share the most convenient spots with super friendly prices, so your trip is complete without burning a hole in your wallet.

## 🛒 Where To Shop Cheap & Convenient In Vung Tau?

Besides familiar supermarkets like Co.opmart, Vung Tau also has little "hidden markets" full of interesting finds at extremely soft prices. Here are two "secret spots" locals love:

### 📍 Thanh Lap Self-Service Store: A "Heaven" Of Affordable Household Goods

![(Thanh Lap Self-Service Store)](https://global-uploads.webflow.com/60af8c708c6f35480d067652/61d270f9c96c942a9d92a492_screenshot_1641181320.png "Thanh Lap Self-Service Store")

* **Address:** 11 Kha Van Can, Ward 7, Vung Tau
* **Why we love it:** It truly has *everything* you need for a little homestay kitchen or daily essentials — from fish sauce, salt, sugar, flour and fresh veggies (when available) to snacks, drinks, and small household items like bowls, plates, spoons and forks. The prices? No contest — much cheaper than supermarkets!

### 📍 Minh Chau Self-Service Store: A Wide Range Of Choices

![(Minh Chau Self-Service Store)](https://global-uploads.webflow.com/60af8c708c6f35480d067652/61bd3beadef11beaa4f06a26_screenshot_1639791566.png "Minh Chau Self-Service Store")

* **Address:** 63-67 Doi Can, Ward 8, Vung Tau
* **Why we love it:** Need more options? Minh Chau is a great suggestion. Beyond household goods and food, it also carries personal care items like detergent and body wash. Bonus: if you want to "chill", they have all kinds of beer at very attractive prices!

## ✨ Why Shop At Local Stores?

* **💰 Budget-friendly prices:** Definitely — you'll save a decent amount compared to big supermarkets.
* **📦 Wide product range:** Don't think small stores have nothing! Here you'll find all kinds of seasonings, fresh ingredients, snacks, drinks, household items, detergent, body wash... nothing is missing.
* **🤝 Support locals:** Shopping here is also a way to support the local economy.

So with the tips above, we hope you have a fun and affordable trip to Vung Tau. Find your favorite homestay and enjoy amazing experiences in this beautiful seaside city!
