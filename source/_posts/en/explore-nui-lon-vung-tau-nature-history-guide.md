# Explore Nui Lon Vung Tau: The Ideal Destination for Nature and History Lovers 🌄

Nui Lon (Big Mountain), also known as Nui Vi Ba, Dai Son or Tuong Ky, is a breathtaking gift of nature to Vung Tau. Rising 254 meters above sea level and covering 400 hectares, Nui Lon stands out not only for its majestic natural scenery but also for its deep historical and cultural value.

## Beauty and Top Attractions on Nui Lon 🏞️

### 1. Thich Ca Phat Dai Pagoda 🛕

!["Thich Ca Phat Dai Pagoda"](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/04/go-and-share-thich-ca-phat-dai-vung-tau-14.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Thich Ca Phat Dai Pagoda")

Thich Ca Phat Dai Pagoda is a Buddhist architectural complex of the Southern (Theravada) school that attracts millions of visitors every year. The pagoda is divided into three main areas, from the peaceful tam quan gate and flower garden to the souvenir display area and the Phat Tich garden.

### 2. Statue of Our Lady of Bai Dau 🙏

The Statue of Our Lady of Bai Dau is an important religious landmark in Vung Tau, featuring a 25-meter statue of the Virgin Mary holding baby Jesus. It's a place where visitors can take part in many events of the Vietnamese Church.

### 3. Ho May Park Tourist Area 🎢

!["Ho May Park Tourist Area"](https://imagevietnam.vnanet.vn//MediaUpload/Org/2023/08/08/homay-208-13-51-5.jpg "Ho May Park Tourist Area")

Ho May Park sits on the summit of Nui Lon at an altitude of more than 210 meters, offering fantastic fun and entertainment experiences. With its vast spaces and mild climate, Ho May Park is nicknamed Vung Tau's "second Da Lat".

### 4. Bach Dinh Villa 🏰

Bach Dinh (White Villa) is a historic villa built in European architectural style, famous for its romantic and refined beauty. Perched on the slopes of Nui Lon, Bach Dinh offers a stunning view over the vast sea and sky of Vung Tau.

## How to Get Up Nui Lon 🚶‍♂️

### 1. Vi Ba Road 🚵‍♂️

Vi Ba Road is the popular choice for visitors traveling to Nui Lon by motorbike or on foot. The road winds dreamily through the scenery, but it isn't suitable for cars due to its many narrow curves.

### 2. Alley 444 Tran Phu 🏞️

Alley 444 Tran Phu is a less-traveled road, but it's short and convenient, and its beautiful mountain-and-sea scenery makes it perfect for photo shoots.

### 3. Cable Car at Ho May Park 🚡

The cable car is the ideal option for those who don't want to spend too much energy. Cable car tickets cost 100,000 VND for adults and 50,000 VND for children.

## The Ideal Time to Explore 🌦️

The best time to visit Nui Lon is from November to April of the following year, when Vung Tau enters its dry season with gentle sunshine, clear blue skies and soft breezes — perfect conditions for your trip.

## Signature Food at Nui Lon 🍦

### Enjoy Vung Tau Yaourt

!["Vung Tau Yaourt"](https://static.riviu.vn/image/2020/06/16/2afafa713a19bd7a29fe27fc707c4df1_output.jpeg "Vung Tau Yaourt")

Vung Tau yaourt (yogurt) is an irresistible snack with the city's signature flavor. Its fragrant, pleasantly sweet-and-creamy taste will satisfy every foodie.

## Travel Tips for Nui Lon Vi Ba 📸

- Check the weather forecast before you go.
- Pack a pair of sneakers if you plan to hike.
- Bring a camera to capture your memories.
- Eat at the restaurants inside Ho May Park tourist area.

## Attractions Near Nui Lon Vi Ba 🗺️

### 1. Christ the King Statue ✝️

Standing 32 meters tall with an arm span of 18 meters, the Christ the King Statue is a symbol of Vung Tau. From here, you can admire a panoramic, picture-perfect view of the city.

### 2. Vung Tau Lighthouse 🌅

The Vung Tau Lighthouse is a striking architectural landmark with more than a century of history. It's also a popular check-in spot among young travelers.
