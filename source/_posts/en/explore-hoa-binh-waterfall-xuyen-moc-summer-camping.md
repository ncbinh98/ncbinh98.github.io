### Explore Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc – A Cool Summer Camping Paradise 🌊🏕️

Looking for a wild, cool escape from the heat right near Vung Tau? We recommend Hoa Binh Waterfall in Xuyen Moc – a hidden gem tucked away among the mountains and forests, where you can camp overnight, swim in the stream and let your soul drift along with the babbling water and chirping birds. Let's explore it together!

---

#### 1. Hoa Binh Waterfall – Nature's Symphony 🏞️

Located in Xuyen Moc district, Ba Ria – Vung Tau province, Hoa Binh Waterfall embodies mother nature at her finest, combining forested mountains, greenery and babbling streams. The water is crystal clear and refreshingly cool, cascading down huge rocks to create a picture both majestic and deeply poetic.

![Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc seen from afar](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2.jpg "Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc amid the forest")

![The cool stream at Hoa Binh Waterfall](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/3.jpg "The crystal-clear stream at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc")

We have to admit, just standing still for a moment melts away all the stress of city life. The sound of flowing water, birdsong and the chatter of visitors blend into a wonderful symphony, inviting every adventurous soul to come and explore.

---

#### 2. The Best Time to Visit Hoa Binh Waterfall 🌤️

In our opinion, the ideal time is early summer – late March to early April. The weather is cool, the sun is gentle, and the water level in the large rock pools is just right, so you can comfortably go down to the foot of the waterfall to explore and take great photos.

![Hoa Binh Waterfall in summer](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/4.jpg "The cool water of Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc in summer")

The cool stream not only nourishes the lush greenery but also serves as the perfect "medicine" to help you leave the city behind, return to nature, bathe in the stream and recharge for the new week.

![Swimming in the stream at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/5.jpg "Cool stream swimming at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc")

---

#### 3. The One-of-a-Kind Overnight Camping Experience ⛺

A huge plus at Hoa Binh Waterfall is that you're allowed to camp overnight right in the middle of nature! Tent areas and Mongolian yurts are set up solidly along the stream bank, with cool water in front of you and endless forest behind.

![Camping tents at Hoa Binh Waterfall](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/6.jpg "Camping tents along the stream at Hoa Binh Waterfall")

![Mongolian yurt at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/7.jpg "Mongolian yurt at the Hoa Binh Waterfall campsite")

- ⛺ **Small tent:** fits 2 people, private yet close to nature
- 🏕️ **Square Mongolian yurt:** holds up to 10 people, perfect for lively group trips with friends
- 💸 **Rental price:** from 600,000 to 2,000,000 VND per night depending on the number of guests; weekday rates are even more reasonable

Just open the tent door and a true "healing" paradise lies right in front of you!

![Relaxation space at Hoa Binh Waterfall](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/8.jpg "Camping space with stream views at Hoa Binh Waterfall")

---

#### 4. Hearty Rustic Cuisine 🍢

Another highlight of Hoa Binh Waterfall is its diverse and tempting food. You can bring your own food or order from the available menu – from aromatic grilled dishes to rustic local flavors, guaranteed to satisfy every taste and every age.

![Food at Hoa Binh Waterfall](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/9.jpg "Fragrant grilled dishes at the Hoa Binh Waterfall campsite")

- 🍖 **Grilled dishes:** so fragrant they're irresistible, and even better enjoyed in the fresh air
- 🥘 **Rustic dishes:** rich with hometown flavors, perfect for every family member
- 📞 **Note:** if you visit on a weekend, remember to contact the kitchen team directly for detailed advice and to pre-order your food!

![A meal at the Hoa Binh Waterfall campsite](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/10.jpg "A rustic meal at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc")

---

#### 5. Family-Bonding Activities 🎣

Besides enjoying the camping space and the food, the Hoa Binh Waterfall area offers plenty of other fun activities:

- 🎣 **Fishing:** bring your fishing rod and try your luck right in front of your tent
- 🦪 **Scooping hen mussels:** catch hen mussels with your own hands and cook them yourself – a great way to teach kids practical life skills while bringing the whole family closer together

![Fishing at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/11.jpg "Fishing activity at Hoa Binh Waterfall")

![Scooping hen mussels in the stream at Hoa Binh Waterfall](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/12.jpg "Hen mussel scooping experience at Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc")

The feeling of catching hen mussels yourself and cooking them on the spot is truly fun and brings you so close to nature!

---

#### 6. Tips for Camping at Hoa Binh Waterfall 📝

For a perfect camping trip, keep these tips in mind:

- 🎫 Book your tent in advance to guarantee your spot, especially on weekends or holidays
- 🎒 Pack the essentials: clothes, toiletries, sunscreen and insect repellent
- 🎣 Bring a fishing rod if you want to try catching and grilling your own fish by the stream (the campsite also rents rods to guests)
- 🍽️ Check the menu in advance and order set meals – they suit large groups and let you sample more dishes
- 🧹 If you cook directly in front of your tent, keep things clean and tidy everything up before checkout

![Hoa Binh Waterfall Xuyen Moc campsite](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/13.jpg "Overview of the Hoa Binh Waterfall campsite")

Hoa Binh Waterfall in Xuyen Moc district, Ba Ria – Vung Tau province is not just a destination; it's a journey of self-discovery and pure enjoyment of nature's pristine beauty.
