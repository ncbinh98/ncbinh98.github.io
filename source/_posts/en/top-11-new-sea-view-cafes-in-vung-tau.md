# Top 11 Newly Opened Sea View Cafes In Vung Tau ✨🌊

Planning a trip to Vung Tau and looking for newly opened cafes to enjoy coffee with a gorgeous sea view? Jot these places down right now so you can bring home some stunning photos!

## 1. SEA & SUN COFFEE 3 ☕

📍 47 Trần Phú, Phường 1, Vũng Tàu

![SEA & SUN COFFEE 3](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/h18-1.jpeg "SEA & SUN COFFEE 3")

SEA & SUN COFFEE 3 is one of the newly opened cafes with a fantastic sea view. Here, you can enjoy great coffee and watch the sunset over the sea.

## 2. OCEAN HOUSE 🌅

📍 63 Trần Phú, Phường 5, Thành phố Vũng Tàu

![OCEAN HOUSE](https://mia.vn/media/uploads/blog-du-lich/Ocean-house-vung-tau-nha-hang-ngam-hoang-hon-sieu-dep-04-1634051712.jpg "OCEAN HOUSE")

OCEAN HOUSE offers an airy, cool space with a romantic sea view. It's an ideal place to relax and soak in the seaside atmosphere.

## 3. OASIS SEA Coffee & Tea 🏖️

📍 63 Trần Phú, Phường 5, Thành phố Vũng Tàu

![OASIS SEA Coffee & Tea](https://123vungtau.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/355282800_227252696854233_5633254804274074470_n-2-1024x768.webp "OASIS SEA Coffee & Tea")

OASIS SEA Coffee & Tea is a cafe combined with tea, offering a peaceful space and a stunning sea view.

## 4. COFFEE SUỐI BÊN BIỂN 🌊

📍 1a Trần Phú, Phường 1, Vũng Tàu

![COFFEE SUỐI BÊN BIỂN](https://anchoivungtau.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ca-phe-suoi-ben-bien-2.jpg "COFFEE SUỐI BÊN BIỂN")

COFFEE SUỐI BÊN BIỂN brings you close to nature and the sea. It's an ideal spot to relax and enjoy delicious coffee.

## 5. THE HILL COFFEE 🌅

📍 2/18 Hải Đăng, Vũng Tàu

![THE HILL COFFEE](https://123vungtau.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-18-1024x768.png "THE HILL COFFEE")

THE HILL COFFEE sits on a high hill, offering a gorgeous sea view from above. The cafe space is airy and comfortable.

📍 01 Lê Quý Đôn, Vũng Tàu

## 7. LOVE BEACH 🌊

📍 35 Trần Phú, Tp Vũng Tàu

## 8. NALI MOUNTAIN VIEW 🏖️

📍 10A Hải Đăng, Vũng Tàu
