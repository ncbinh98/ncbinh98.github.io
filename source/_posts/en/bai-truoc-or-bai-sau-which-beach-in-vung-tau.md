# Travelling to Vung Tau: Bai Truoc or Bai Sau, Which Beach to Choose? 🏖️

## Exploring Vung Tau's Bai Truoc: Peaceful & Relaxing 🌅
![Bai Truoc](https://2trip.vn/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/du-lich-vung-tau-nen-chon-bai-truoc-hay-bai-sau.jpg "Bai Truoc")
Bai Truoc lies on Quang Trung Street, the center of Vung Tau's seaside road. With its "peaceful" beauty, Bai Truoc is like a small sea bay embraced by the two mountains Nui Lon (Big Mountain) and Nui Nho (Small Mountain), with clear blue water and gentle waves. It's the ideal place to gaze at the sea and enjoy fresh seafood at spots like Hai san Thanh Phat, Ganh Hao, quán Tre (Bamboo restaurant), and to explore nearby attractions such as Bach Dinh (White Villa), the Vung Tau cable car, the Ho May tourist area, and Bai Truoc park.

## Exploring Vung Tau's Bai Sau: Energetic & Bustling 🌊
![Bai Sau](https://2trip.vn/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/du-lich-vung-tau-nen-chon-bai-truoc-hay-bai-sau1.jpg "Bai Sau")
Bai Sau, located on Thuy Van Street, is the ideal beach for those who love the hustle and bustle and want to swim in the sea. With a 5km-long shoreline, no hidden reefs and clear blue water, Bai Sau is always crowded with tourists. In the evening, the space glitters with lights, and it's also a wonderful place to watch the sunrise. Near Bai Sau, visitors can explore Doi Con Heo (Pig Hill), the Christ the King statue, the Heaven's Gate at Mui Nghinh Phong, and enjoy seafood at places like Lang nuong Song Phung (Song Phung Grill Village), Trong Tin, Yogurt Rainbow, and the CSJ restaurant.

## Should You Choose Bai Truoc or Bai Sau? 🏝️
Depending on your purpose and personal preferences, you can choose Bai Truoc or Bai Sau when travelling to Vung Tau. Bai Truoc suits those who want to relax and enjoy the scenery, while Bai Sau is the ideal destination for those who love swimming and a lively atmosphere.
