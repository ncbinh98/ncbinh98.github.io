# Vietnam Sea & Island Festival – Vung Tau City 2024 🌊

<img src="https://drscdn.500px.org/photo/1094005433/q%3D80_m%3D600/v2?sig=2a84a751be9f9ca33e0085965bbd775b2f9ae4cd5f52bcc01abb36e6caddd739" alt="sunbeam-homestay-vt"/>

The Vietnam Sea & Island Festival – Vung Tau City 2024 will take place from **May 23 to 26, 2024** on Quang Trung Street, Bai Truoc, Vung Tau City. The event aims to introduce, honor and promote the cultural and natural beauty of Vietnam's seas and islands.

The event is organized by the Phan Trong Binh Fund for Heroes of the People's Armed Forces, with the goal of introducing, honoring and preserving coastal cultural heritage values, and boosting domestic and international tourism.

According to the organizers, the Vietnam Sea & Island Festival – Vung Tau City 2024 is designed as a series of diverse events, introducing residents and visitors to the strategic role, position and potential of Vietnam's islands, and the cultural values of coastal regions.

## Festival Highlights

The highlight of the festival is the art night **"Hai trinh khat vong" (Journey of Aspiration)** on Saturday evening **May 25** at the stage on Quang Trung Street square. The show features more than **700 dancers, actors and artists**, including diva My Linh, Ho Ngoc Ha, Phuong Linh, Trong Hieu, Nguyen Ngoc Anh, Phuong My Chi...

The "Journey of Aspiration" art show will be **broadcast live** on Vietnam Television and rebroadcast by Ba Ria – Vung Tau Television so visitors far away can also watch.

## Vietnam Sea & Island Festival 2024 Program

Other events at the Vietnam Sea & Island Festival 2024 include:

- **Food fair** (May 23-26)
- **The Luminous Harmony Show** minishow (evenings of May 23 and 24)
- **Mass yoga festival** (morning of May 24)
- **Vietnamese Ao Dai parade** (afternoon of May 24)
- **Mobile propaganda team for Vietnam seas and islands** (morning of May 24)

According to a representative of the Phan Trong Binh Fund, this program aims to build an impressive and meaningful sea and island festival model that will become a regular, annual coastal cultural exchange event nationwide. The event is also planned to rotate among coastal provinces across the country — a great opportunity for local cooperation and joint efforts to preserve Vietnam's seas and islands.

## More Information

Organizations, businesses and residents interested in the events can learn more and register for activities in the plan of the Vietnam Sea & Island Festival 2024 organizing committee.
