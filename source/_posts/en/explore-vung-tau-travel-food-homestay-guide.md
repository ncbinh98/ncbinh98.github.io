# Explore Vung Tau: A Wonderful Journey Of Travel, Food And Homestays

## About Vung Tau

<img src="https://bcp.cdnchinhphu.vn/334894974524682240/2023/12/18/ba-ria-vung-tau-1-1702878003826817104374.jpg" alt="sunbeam-homestay-vt"/>

Vung Tau, a beautiful seaside city about 2 hours' drive from Saigon, is the ideal destination for anyone wanting to escape the city's hustle and bustle. Vung Tau isn't just famous for its long beaches and clear blue water — it also charms visitors with rich local food and dreamy homestays.

## Vung Tau Travel: Must-Visit Spots

### Bai Sau

Bai Sau is Vung Tau's most famous beach, with fine white sand and gentle waves. It's perfect for swimming, flying kites and water sports. Seafood restaurants line the beach, serving fresh dishes.

### Bai Truoc

Bai Truoc has a calmer beauty — ideal for strolling, watching sunsets and enjoying fresh air. Parks, cafes and restaurants surround it, creating a poetic and romantic setting.

### Christ The King Statue

The Christ the King statue, Vung Tau's icon, stands on top of Nui Nho (Small Mountain). Visitors can climb 800 steps to the top and admire the 32-meter statue. From here you get a stunning panoramic view of Vung Tau.

### Vung Tau Lighthouse

The Vung Tau Lighthouse sits on Nui Nho and was built during the French colonial era. One of Vietnam's oldest lighthouses, it offers fresh air and a full view of the city.

## Vung Tau Food: Flavors Of The Sea

### Banh Khot

Banh Khot is Vung Tau's signature dish. Tiny crispy pancakes topped with fresh shrimp, served with raw vegetables and sweet-sour fish sauce — an unforgettable flavor.

### Fresh Seafood

Vung Tau is famous for fresh seafood. Enjoy grilled squid, steamed scallops and cheese-grilled lobster at beachside restaurants. The Vung Tau night market is the perfect spot for delicious seafood at reasonable prices.

### Ray Fish Hotpot

Ray fish hotpot is a must-try in Vung Tau. The rich sour-spicy broth combined with fresh ray fish makes a hearty, satisfying dish.

## Conclusion

Vung Tau is an ideal destination for all ages — from sea lovers and culture explorers to food enthusiasts. With attractive sights, delicious food and unique homestays, Vung Tau will surely bring you wonderful experiences. Pack your backpack and explore Vung Tau today!
