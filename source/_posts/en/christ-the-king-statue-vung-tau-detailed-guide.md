# Christ the King Statue Vung Tau - Detailed Guide and Tips

## The Christ the King Statue Vung Tau - A Must-Visit Landmark

### 1. Exploring the Christ the King Statue
![tuong_chua_kito](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/01/tuong-dai-chua-kito-vua-1.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "The Christ the King Statue")

#### Where is the Christ the King Statue located?
📍 The Christ the King Statue, also known as the statue of Christ with open arms, is the symbol of Vung Tau city, standing atop Small Mountain (Tao Phung Mountain) on Thuy Van Street.

#### The history of building the Christ with open arms statue
📜 The statue was built starting in 1972, inaugurated in 1994, and recognized as a national-level historical-cultural relic.

#### How tall is the Christ the King Statue?
📏 The statue is 32 meters tall, situated at an altitude of 136 meters above sea level, with arms stretched 18.4 meters wide.

### 2. How to Get to the Christ the King Statue
![di_chuyen_vung_tau](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/01/tuong-dai-chua-kito-vua-3.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Getting to Vung Tau")

#### How to get to Vung Tau
🛣️ Vung Tau city is about 100km from Ho Chi Minh City. You can travel by motorbike, car, coach, or hydrofoil.

#### How to get to the Christ the King Statue
🛵 From Vung Tau city center, it takes about 10 minutes by motorbike or taxi, costing around 70,000 - 100,000 VND.

### 3. Tips for Visiting the Christ the King Statue Vung Tau
![tham_quan_tuong_chua](https://ik.imagekit.io/tvlk/blog/2023/01/tuong-dai-chua-kito-vua-5-819x1024.jpg?tr=dpr-2,w-675 "Visiting the Christ the King Statue")

#### What are the opening hours of the Christ with open arms statue?
🕖 Opening hours are from 07:00 AM to 05:00 PM, from Monday to Sunday.

#### How much is the entrance fee to visit the statue?
💸 Admission is free; parking fee is by donation.

#### When is the best time to visit the Christ the King Statue?
🌞 It's best to go in the dry season (November to April) and in the early morning or late afternoon to avoid the intense heat.

#### The journey to conquer the Christ the King Statue
🚶‍♂️ Climb 811 steps, stop to rest at the stations, then continue climbing 133 spiral steps to reach the arms of Christ.

#### What should you bring when visiting the Christ the King Statue?
🎒 Bring a sun-protection jacket, bottled water, comfortable yet modest clothing, and avoid carrying heavy items.
